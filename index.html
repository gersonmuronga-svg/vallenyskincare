Good to know

Questions

How do I place an order?

Add products to your bag and tap "Order on WhatsApp". Your order is sent to us as a message, and we'll confirm availability, delivery and payment with you directly.

Do you deliver?

Yes — we deliver within Windhoek and send to other towns across Namibia via courier. Delivery is free in Windhoek on orders over N$ 800.

How do I pay?

We accept EFT, eWallet / bank-app transfers and cash on collection. Payment details are shared when we confirm your order.

Which products are right for my skin?

Send us a message with your main concern (breakouts, dark spots, dryness, dullness) and we'll recommend a routine for you — free of charge.

How soon will I see results?

Most clients notice smoother, brighter skin within 2–4 weeks, with bigger changes to dark spots and texture over 6–12 weeks of consistent use.

Ready for your glow-up?

Chat with us for personal recommendations, order questions or to collect in person. We usually reply within the hour.
